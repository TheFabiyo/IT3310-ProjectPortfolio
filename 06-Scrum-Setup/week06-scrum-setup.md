## Agile Delivery Fit

The patient intake and scheduling workflow could benefit from iterative delivery because clinic staff can test early versions and give feedback on what works and what needs to change. This would allow the team to make improvements before the system is fully implemented across all six clinics. Staff training would need stronger advance planning because employees at all six clinics need to be available and prepared before the system is rolled out.

---

## Scrum Team

**Product Owner:** NovaTech Product Manager

**Scrum Master:** NovaTech team member trained in Scrum

**Developers:** Software developers, system analysts, and testers

### Rationale

I think these roles make sense because everyone has a different responsibility. The Product Owner focuses on what is most valuable to RCHN and manages the Product Backlog. The Scrum Master helps the team work effectively and work through problems without taking over their responsibilities. The Developers plan their Sprint work and focus on creating and testing a usable part of the system that meets the Definition of Done.

---

## Sprint 1 Plan

**Sprint Goal:** Create a basic version of the patient intake and scheduling workflow that clinic staff can review and provide feedback on before further development.

**Course Planning Limit:** 25 points

### Selected Backlog Items

- Scheduling Workflow Prototype, High Priority, 13 points
- Intake Form Standardization, High Priority, 8 points
- Sprint Communication Update, Medium Priority, 2 points

**Total Course Planning Points Selected:** 23

---

## Sprint Planning Reflection

I chose these items because they focus on improving the main intake and scheduling process for RCHN. The scheduling prototype and intake form standardization are both high priority, and they give clinic staff something to review and provide feedback on. The communication update also helps keep everyone informed about our progress. Together, these items total 23 points, which keeps us under the 25-point limit without adding unnecessary work. I think the team needs to learn whether the new workflow actually makes things easier for clinic staff before moving on to other features. I decided to leave out the Dashboard Reporting Draft because it would be better to get the main system working first before focusing on additional reporting features.

---

## Scrum Master Reflection

### Obstacle and Response

One problem that could slow down the Sprint is getting feedback from clinic staff at all six locations. If employees are too busy or unavailable, the team might have trouble knowing if the new workflow actually meets their needs. The Scrum Master could help by working with RCHN to arrange feedback sessions and making sure the team has what they need. The Developers would still be responsible for deciding how to complete their work.

### Preparing for Week 7

This week's Sprint Planning helped me understand that not everything can be done at once, even if it seems important. Next week, I can use what we learned to organize the Product Backlog based on what provides the most value to RCHN. I also think feedback from clinic staff will help us figure out which features should come first and which ones can wait until later.
