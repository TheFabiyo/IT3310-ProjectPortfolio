# Project Plan Foundation: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System

**Consultant:** Fabian Rodriguez  
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Project Manager, NovaTech Consulting  
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)  
**Date:** October 1, 2026  

---

## Scope

### In Scope, Phase One

* Patient intake workflow standardization across all six clinics
* Appointment scheduling system replacement
* Staff training for the new system at all six locations
* Initial rollout support for 90 days post launch

### Out of Scope, Phase One

* Patient facing online scheduling portal
* Billing system integration with the new scheduling data
* Executive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership
* Mobile application for clinic staff

### Scope Justification

I would leave the patient facing online scheduling portal out of phase one because the main focus is replacing and improving the intake and scheduling system used across the six RCHN clinics. Adding a patient portal would expand the project beyond that main goal and could require additional development, testing, resources, and support. It could still be considered in a later phase after the new intake and scheduling system is successfully implemented.

---

## SMART Objectives

### Objective 1

By the end of the approved pilot period all six RCHN clinics will complete the agreed patient intake and scheduling workflow using the replacement system as verified by sponsor approved acceptance criteria.

**Connection to Product Vision:** This supports the product vision by creating a more consistent intake and scheduling process across all six RCHN clinics.

### Objective 2

By the end of the approved implementation period staff at all six RCHN clinics will complete training for the new intake and scheduling system based on sponsor approved training requirements.

**Connection to Product Vision:** Staff training supports the product vision by helping clinic staff use the new system consistently and complete intake and scheduling tasks more reliably.

---

## Planning Constraints

### Constraint 1

The replacement system must support patient intake and scheduling across all six RCHN clinic locations.

**Effect on Planning:** The project plan must account for implementation needs across six locations including workflow coordination training and rollout support.

### Constraint 2

Phase one is limited to the approved patient intake and scheduling system replacement and its confirmed scope.

**Effect on Planning:** Additional features outside the current scope would need to be evaluated and approved before adding them because they could affect schedule resources cost and project complexity.

---

## Assumptions

**Assumption 1:** RCHN staff at all six clinics will be available for training before the new system is implemented. If this is not true, the training and rollout schedule may need to be adjusted.

**Assumption 2:** The six clinics will be able to use a standardized patient intake and scheduling workflow. If major differences between clinic workflows are discovered, the project scope and implementation plan may need to change.

---

## Planning Foundation Synthesis

My Week 3 product vision helped guide the decision to focus the project on creating a simpler and more consistent intake and scheduling process across all six clinics. I had to decide which features should be included in phase one without expanding the project too far. The final scope, schedule, and acceptance criteria still need sponsor confirmation before these planning decisions should be considered final.
