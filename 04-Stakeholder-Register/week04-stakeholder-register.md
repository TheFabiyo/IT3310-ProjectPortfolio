## Engagement Strategy Reflection

### Highest Priority Stakeholder
I think the RCHN Chief Medical Officer requires the most attention because they have both high interest and high influence over the project. They need to make sure the new system improves patient intake and scheduling without creating problems for clinic operations. If their concerns were overlooked, the project could lose support or move forward with a system that does not meet RCHN's needs.

### Risk of Omission
One group I considered but did not include was community partner organizations that refer patients to RCHN. I left them out because they are not as directly involved with the new intake and scheduling system as the stakeholders I included. I would add them later if we learned that the new system changes the referral process or creates issues with how outside organizations send patient information to RCHN.
