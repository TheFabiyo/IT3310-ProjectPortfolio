# User Persona and Product Vision: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System

**Consultant:** Fabian Rodriguez
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Product Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** September 16, 2026

---

## User Persona

**Name:** Maria
**Role:** Intake Coordinator
**Years in Role:** 3 – 7 years
**Tech Comfort Level:** Medium, comfortable with common tools

---

### Primary Work Goal

Get patients checked in and scheduled quickly and accurately.

---

### Main Frustration with the Current System

The current system has too many screens, and it can be hard to tell if an appointment was actually saved.

---

### What Would Make Their Work Better

A simpler and more consistent system that makes intake and scheduling easier and clearly confirms when information has been saved.

---

## Product Vision Statement

For RCHN intake coordinators who need a simpler and more reliable way to complete patient intake and scheduling, a unified patient intake and scheduling system will provide a more consistent and reliable workflow across RCHN clinics.

---

### Rationale

I chose intake coordinators because the research shows that they have to use too many screens to complete a patient registration. The research also shows that staff sometimes cannot confirm whether an appointment was saved successfully. The intended outcome is a more consistent and reliable workflow across RCHN clinics.
