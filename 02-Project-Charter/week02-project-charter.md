---
## Project Title
RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System Replacement
---
## Project Sponsor
Marcus Webb
---
## Business Need
RCHN currently uses an aging patient intake and scheduling system across its clinics. The current system creates scheduling delays and inconsistent workflows between locations, making it harder for staff to provide  reliable and consistent process. Replacing the system is important because we need a more dependable platform that better supports patient intake, scheduling, and the staff who use it everyday.
---
## Purpose and High-Level Scope
The purpose of this project is to replace RCHN's aging patient intake and scheduling system with a more reliable system that supports consistent workflows across all six clinics. The project will focus on patient intake, appointment scheduling, staff training, and support for the initial rollout. Detailed features outside these areas will be addressed separately if they are approved for the project.
---
## Success Criteria
1. All six RCHN clinics successfully transition to the approved patient intake and scheduling system.
2. Designated staff complete the required training and are able to use the new system.
3. Patient intake and appointment scheduling can be completed consistently and accurately across the six clinics.
